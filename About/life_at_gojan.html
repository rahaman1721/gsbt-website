Life @ Gojan

Gojan School of Business and Technology is a premier institution dedicated to empowering lives through wisdom. Our heritage of excellence, dating back to 2005, continues to shape the innovators of tomorrow across our expansive 80-acre green campus.

A Campus That Shapes Future Leaders

Established in 2005, Gojan School of Business and Technology is nestled on a lush, pollution-free 80-acre campus in Edapalayam, Redhills, Chennai. We offer 14 Undergraduate and 1 Postgraduate programme, committed to academic excellence and holistic student development.
