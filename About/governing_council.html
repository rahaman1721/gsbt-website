Governing Council Board

The Governing Council provides strategic direction and oversight to ensure the institution maintains the highest standards of academic excellence, governance, and institutional development.

About the Governing Council

The Governing Council of Gojan School of Business and Technology comprises distinguished academicians, industry leaders, and subject matter experts who bring diverse perspectives and expertise to guide the institution's strategic initiatives and ensure quality education delivery.

The Council meets regularly to review academic programs, approve policies, oversee infrastructure development, and ensure the institution's alignment with national education policies and industry requirements.
