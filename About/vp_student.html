Vice-President (Student Affairs)

Nurturing personal growth and building a vibrant campus community for a well-rounded student experience.

Mr. RAMADHURAI R

Vice-President (Student Affairs)

Greetings! As the Vice-President of Student Affairs, I extend a warm welcome to our Gojan. Your journey here is not just about academics, but also about personal growth and a vibrant campus life. We are committed to providing a supportive environment where you can thrive both academically and personally. From orientation programs to career guidance, we offer a range of services to help you navigate your college experience. Our dedicated team is here to listen, guide, and address your concerns. We also organise a variety of extracurricular activities, clubs, and events to enhance your social and leadership skills. Together, let's create unforgettable memories and shape a well-rounded future for you at our Gojan.
