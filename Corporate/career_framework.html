Career Framework

A comprehensive roadmap for student success, integrating skill development, vocational training, and strategic career planning.

Goals & Objectives

  • Foster employability excellence through curated training in aptitude, communication, and technical domains.
  • Build enduring industry synergies for internships, projects, and placements.
  • Facilitate on-campus hiring, leadership conclaves, and career acceleration programs.
  • Ensure 100% placement support for eligible aspirants through continuous mentorship.
  • Inspire a culture of professional ethics, innovation, and lifelong career evolution.

HIGHLIGHTS

Year-wise Aptitude Modules

Step-by-step skill-building in quant, logic & reasoning.

Soft Skill Training

Communication, interviews, GDs & presentations.

Technical Training

Industry-relevant curriculum tailored per department.

Company-Specific Coaching

Tailored prep for TCS, Infosys, Wipro & more.

Year-Wise Training Modules

Foundation Stage

1st Year

Build a strong base in aptitude and communication; initiate exposure to technical fundamentals.

Aptitude

  • Number Systems & Divisibility
  • HCF & LCM, Simplification
  • Percentages, Averages
  • Logical Reasoning & Vedic Maths

Soft Skills

  • Verbal & Written Communication
  • Grammar & Vocabulary Enrichment
  • Listening & Reading Practice
  • Phonetics, Language Lab

Technical

  • Dept-wise Core Concepts
  • Basic Computer Applications
  • Technical Communication
Application Stage

2nd Year

Strengthen logical thinking, introduce business communication, and advance domain knowledge.

Aptitude

  • Ratio, Proportion, Profit & Loss
  • Simple & Compound Interest
  • Time-Speed-Distance, Work
  • Puzzles & Data Interpretation

Soft Skills

  • Business Email & Memo Writing
  • Interview Basics & GDs
  • Public Speaking Fundamentals
  • Excellence in Role Plays

Technical

  • Domain-specific Tool Intro
  • Technical Writing & Docs
  • Case Study & Mini Projects
Placement Readiness

3rd Year

Align training with company expectations; simulate real-time interview environments.

Aptitude

  • Advanced DI (Charts & Caselets)
  • Permutations & Combinations
  • Probability & TCS NQT Mocks
  • Product Company Prep (CVQ)

Soft Skills

  • Advanced GD & Interviews
  • Crisis Communication & EI
  • Leadership Messaging
  • Corporate Social Media

Technical

  • Coding & Domain Assessments
  • Industry Tool Mini Projects
  • Recruiter Specific Simulations
Pre-Placement Execution

4th Year

Prepare students for actual placement processes with precision and strategy.

Aptitude

  • Company-specific Test Series
  • Final Revision Strategies
  • Aptitude Simulations & Analytics
  • Placement Performance Tracking

Soft Skills

  • Advanced Mock Interviews
  • Capstone Portfolio Design
  • Ethical Communication
  • Entrepreneurial Skills

Technical

  • Final Year Project Learning
  • Industry-led Mentorship
  • Role-based Assessments
