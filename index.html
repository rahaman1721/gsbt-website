About Us

The name "GOJAN" carries a legacy deeper than syllables. It is christened after the founder's parents Gopalakrishnan and Janakiammal a tribute by a dutiful son to his father's lifelong dream: an engineering college that would truly empower young minds. Since 2005, that dream has grown into a vibrant 85 acre campus at Redhills, Chennai, free from pollution and full of possibility. We proudly offer 14 UG & 1 PG programme, affiliated to Anna University, recognized by AICTE, and accredited by NAAC.
