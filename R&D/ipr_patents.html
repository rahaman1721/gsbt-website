IPR / Patents

Protecting innovation and fostering an environment of intellectual property excellence through specialized guidance in patenting and technical rights.

IPR Cell Overview

The Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) Cell at Gojan School of Business and Technology is dedicated to fostering a culture of innovation and protecting the creative works of our faculty and students. We provide guidance and support for identifying, protecting, and managing intellectual property generated through research and development activities.

Our goal is to transform academic research into valuable intellectual assets that contribute to technological advancement and economic growth.

Key Objectives

  • To create awareness about IPR among faculty and students through workshops and seminars.
  • To provide administrative and technical support for filing patents, copyrights, and trademarks.
  • To facilitate the commercialization of intellectual property generated at the institution.
  • To establish a robust framework for managing institutional intellectual property.
  • To encourage collaborative research with industry partners while ensuring fair IP protection.

Patent Filing Process

1

Invention Disclosure

Submit the invention disclosure form detailing technical aspects.

2

Prior Art Search

Conduct a thorough search to ensure novelty.

3

Drafting & Filing

Prepare specification and file with Patent Office.

4

Examination

Address subsequent examinations until grant.
