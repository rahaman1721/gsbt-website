Startups and Incubation

Nurturing entrepreneurial talent and transforming innovative ideas into successful business ventures through strategic mentorship and seed funding.

Overview

The Startups and Incubation Centre at Gojan School of Business and Technology is an initiative to promote entrepreneurship among students and faculty. We aim to transform innovative ideas into commercially viable products or services by providing the necessary support system.

Our incubation centre acts as a catalyst for socio-economic development by nurturing young minds and helping them build sustainable businesses in various technology domains.

Key Objectives

  • To identify and nurture innovative ideas from students and faculty.
  • To provide infrastructure and technical support for prototyping and product development.
  • To offer mentorship from industry experts and successful entrepreneurs.
  • To facilitate networking with investors and venture capitalists for funding.
  • To provide legal and patenting support for startup innovations.

Facilities & Resources

Co-working

High-speed internet, meeting rooms, and presentation facilities.

Prototyping

Advanced facilities for building and testing product prototypes.

Mentorship

Regular interaction with industry veterans and advisors.

Support

Guidance on company registration and legal compliance.

Strategic Collaborations

MOU signed with Aerochitech Company, a leading UAV and its allied parts manufacturer in Chennai, to foster research and development in drone technology.
