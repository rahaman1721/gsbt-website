Our Vibrant Campus Life

At Gojan, learning extends far beyond the lecture halls. Our diverse range of activities is designed to foster leadership, social responsibility, and professional growth in every student.

NSS (National Service Scheme)

Developing personalities through community service. Our NSS volunteers actively engage in rural development, health camps, and social awareness programs.

Learn More

YRC (Youth Red Cross)

Promoting humanitarian principles and health awareness. YRC members lead blood donation drives and disaster risk reduction training.

Learn More

RRC (Red Ribbon Club)

A movement to harness the energy of the youth for social change, focusing on health education and voluntary blood donation society.

Learn More

CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility)

Empowering communities and creating sustainable impact through corporate initiatives and ethical stewardship.

Learn More

UBA (Unnat Bharat Abhiyan)

Connecting Higher Educational Institutions with rural development for a self-reliant India through Science & Technology.

Learn More
