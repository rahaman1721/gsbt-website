About The Symposium

About CHAKRAVYUHA 2K26

CHAKRAVYUHA 2K26 is a One Day National Level Technical Symposium organized by Gojan School of Business and Technology. The symposium provides a premier platform for students to showcase their technical knowledge, creativity, innovation, problem-solving abilities, and emerging technology skills.

The event brings together passionate students from diverse engineering and technology disciplines to participate in a wide spectrum of technical competitions, project presentations, coding challenges, innovative activities, and creative technical events.

With exciting competitions and attractive prizes worth ₹2 Lakhs, CHAKRAVYUHA 2K26 aims to encourage innovation, collaboration, technical excellence, and knowledge sharing among future engineers.

Why Participate in CHAKRAVYUHA 2K26?

Showcase Your Talent

Demonstrate your technical knowledge, creativity and problem-solving skills through exciting competitions.

Compete at the National Level

Interact and compete with students from different institutions and academic backgrounds.

CHAKRAVYUHA 2K26 is open to students from all departments.
