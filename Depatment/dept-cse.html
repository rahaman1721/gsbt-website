About Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering was established in the year 2005. The Department offers a four-years Undergraduate Degree Programme in Computer Science and Engineering and a two-year Postgraduate Degree Programme in Computer Science and Engineering. UG Programme are Permanent Affiliations approved by Anna University, Chennai. Computer Science and Engineering plays a key role in the development of any field of engineering. It is about translating theoretical research into practical solutions and applications that are used by society.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To produce competent software professionals, academicians, researchers and entrepreneurs with moral values through quality education in the field of Computer Science and Engineering.

Mission

  • Enriching the students knowledge and computing skills through an innovative teaching learning process.
  • Developing the entrepreneurship and employable skills of students through adequate industry institute interaction.
  • Inculcating leadership skills, professional communication skills with moral and ethical values to serve the society.

Laboratories

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories that provide an excellent learning environment. These labs offer extensive hands-on experience in various programming languages and emerging technologies.

  • Data Structures Laboratory: Focuses on fundamental data organization and algorithmic efficiency using C/C++.
  • Object Oriented Programming Laboratory: Emphasizes software design principles using Java and C++.
  • Database Management Systems Laboratory: Hands-on training in SQL, database design, and management using MySQL/Oracle.
  • Operating Systems Laboratory: Explores system calls, process scheduling, and memory management in Linux/Unix environments.
  • Data Science Laboratory: Introduction to data analysis, visualization, and statistical modeling using Python/R.
  • Software CASE Tools Laboratory: Focuses on software engineering methodologies and project management tools.
  • Internet Programming Laboratory: Covers web development technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Server-side scripting.
