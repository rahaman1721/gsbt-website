About Department

Welcome to the Department of Robotics and Automation at Gojan School of Business and Technology, Redhills. The department was established in 2024 with an intake of 30 students. We provide a vibrant learning environment supported by well-qualified faculty, enabling students to explore the field of robotics and automation. Our curriculum is designed to offer a strong foundation in the fundamental principles of robotics and automation, while also incorporating the latest advancements in the field.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To develop Robotics Engineers possessing diverse professional abilities and innovative methods for collaboration with industry, research, and entrepreneurship

Mission

  • To provide quality training and Advanced domain knowledge in Robotics and Automation.
  • To empower students to solve complex industrial and societal challenges using advanced technologies.
  • To provide an ambiance for research through collaborations with industry and academia.

Head of Department Message

Mr. Ranjith P, M.E.

Robotics is the future of manufacturing and service industries. Our department is dedicated to providing students with the skills to build the robots of tomorrow.

We provide a collaborative environment that encourages discovery and technical excellence in the field of intelligent machines.

Laboratories

The Department of Robotics and Automation provides students with access to specialized facilities designed for the study of intelligent machines. Our labs focus on the design, simulation, and implementation of robotic systems.

  • Robot Modelling and Simulation Laboratory: Focuses on developing kinematic and dynamic models of robotic manipulators and trajectory planning.
  • Manufacturing Technology Laboratory: Hands-on experience in manufacturing components and understanding machining processes.
  • Industrial Automation Laboratory: Training in programming and operating automation systems for industrial process design.
  • Mechatronics and Control Systems Laboratory: Integration of mechanical systems with electronic control and sensor feedback.
  • Microprocessor and Embedded Systems Laboratory: Study of hardware-software interfacing for robotic control and intelligent systems.

Department News

3D Printing Workshop 20 Weeks ago

On February 18, 2026, a workshop on 3D printing technology was conducted to introduce students to the fundamentals of additive manufacturing. The session included hands-on experience with 3D printing tools and machines, enabling participants to understand real-world applications in mechanical, automation, and robotics domains.
