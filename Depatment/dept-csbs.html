About Department

The B.Tech. in Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) program was established in the academic year 2023. This program is designed to nurture technically skilled and professionally competent graduates who uphold strong ethical values and responsible citizenship.

The curriculum blends core computer science subjects with business processes and a wide range of elective courses. It promotes continuous self-learning and equips students with knowledge of emerging technologies, business practices, and service-oriented approaches essential for modern industry needs.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a center of excellence in Computer Science & Business Systems by providing high-quality technical education with the necessary skills and values to meet the demands of the industry and society.

Mission

  • To create a close-to-perfect place for the learning process by adopting efficient teaching methodologies and strategies in Computer Science & Business Systems.
  • To develop a platform for research by supporting innovative ideas for Research and Development based on the needs of the Industry and Society.
  • To provide career development and higher studies assistance to the student community.
  • To inculcate the demanded skills for modern technologies with ethical and human values.

The fusion of Computer Science and Business Systems is paving the way for a new era of digital transformation. We are committed to shaping professionals who can bridge this gap effectively.

We provide a unique learning environment that fosters both technical mastery and business acumen, preparing our graduates for global leadership roles.

Laboratories

The Department of Computer Science and Business Systems features modern laboratories that provide extensive practical exposure. Our students gain hands-on expertise in technical systems and business intelligence tools.

  • Computational Thinking and Programming Laboratory: Focuses on developing logical reasoning and core programming excellence.
  • Database Management Systems Laboratory: Practical study of data modeling, SQL, and business data architecture.
  • Business Analytics and Intelligence Laboratory: Hands-on experience with data visualization and predictive modeling tools.
  • Software Engineering and Agile Laboratory: Focused on modern software development life cycles and project management.
  • Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Laboratory: Integration of business processes using enterprise-grade software solutions.
  • Financial and Economic Modeling Laboratory: Quantitative analysis of business data and economic trend simulation.
