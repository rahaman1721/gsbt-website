About Department

The Department of Medical Electronics is a specialized discipline that integrates engineering with medical fields and clinical practice. It serves to bridge the gap between engineering and medicine. This field focuses on the study of the basics of medical science and the application of engineering principles such as electronics, computer-based software, and artificial intelligence to develop and manufacture devices for diagnostic and analytical purposes in healthcare. Our distinguished faculty members possess a wealth of expertise in interdisciplinary research areas like Biomedical Signal Processing, Medical Imaging, Biosensors, and Embedded Systems.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To contribute to the society through continuous learning and adapt to evolving technologies and methodologies in Medical Electronics Engineering.

Mission

  • To educate students to become skilled professionals in the field, equipped with the knowledge and competencies required in medical electronics through progressive learning.
  • To focus the students on advancing healthcare, emphasizing the role of Medical Electronics Engineering in the healthcare sector.

Head of Department Message

Ms. Bhuvaneswari J, M.E.

Head of Department

Medical Electronics is a field with deep societal impact. Our department is committed to training engineers who can apply their technical skills to advance healthcare.

We provide a unique learning environment that combines electronic engineering with biological sciences, fostering innovation in the medical field.

Laboratories

The Department of Medical Electronics provides specialized laboratories that integrate engineering principles with medical sciences. Our facilities are designed to give students practical experience in bio-signal analysis, medical instrumentation, and diagnostic technology.

  • Electronics and Microcontroller Laboratory: Focuses on the design and testing of electronic circuits and microcontroller-based medical systems.
  • Digital Signal and Medical Image Processing Laboratory: Practical analysis of bio-signals and medical images using software tools like MATLAB.
  • Biomedical Instrumentation Laboratory: Hands-on training in recording physiological signals like ECG, EEG, and EMG using advanced diagnostic kits.
  • Medical Electronics Laboratory: Specialized study of therapeutic and diagnostic equipment and electrical safety measurements in healthcare.
  • Pathological and Biological Analysis Laboratory: Experimental study of biological samples and clinical analysis techniques related to human health.
