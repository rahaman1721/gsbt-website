About Department

The Department of Information Technology (IT) plays a key role in supporting organizations in today's digital era. IT professionals manage and maintain hardware, software, and networks to ensure smooth and efficient operations. They provide continuous support and troubleshooting to reduce downtime and improve productivity. The department also focuses on data management and security by implementing strong measures to protect sensitive information and ensure reliable data backup and recovery.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To provide a quality education that produces skilled and responsible software professionals, researchers and engineers with a focus on promoting human values and environmentally sustainable practices to make meaningful contributions to both industry and society.

Mission

  • Developing our students with a strong foundation in Computer Science and technical proficiency using innovative teaching and learning methods.
  • Collaborating with IT industry and academicians for achieving quality technical education and contribution to research through active participation of all the stakeholders.
  • Producing IT professionals who are equipped to make contributions to the industry, society and the local community.

We provide a supportive environment that encourages technical mastery and creative problem-solving in the digital domain.

Laboratories

The Department of Information Technology is equipped with specialized laboratories that foster innovation and practical expertise. Our facilities provide students with the tools needed to excel in software development, data analytics, and network management.

  • Problem Solving and Python Programming Laboratory: Foundational programming and logic development.
  • Data Structures and Algorithms Laboratory: Implementation of complex data organization techniques using C++/Java.
  • Object-Oriented Programming Laboratory: Focuses on modular software design and application development.
  • Database Management Systems Laboratory: Practical training in relational database design and SQL querying.
  • Operating Systems Laboratory: Hands-on experience with Linux internals, shell scripting, and kernel concepts.
  • Full Stack Web Development Laboratory: End-to-end web application development using modern stacks (Node.js, React).
  • Mobile Application Development Laboratory: Creating cross-platform mobile apps using Flutter and Android Studio.
  • Data Science Laboratory: Advanced analytics and visualization using Python/R and Jupyter Notebooks.
