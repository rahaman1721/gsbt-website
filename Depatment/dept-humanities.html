About Department

The Department of Science and Humanities has been functioning since 2005 with the aim to provide the students with deep insights into basic sciences and lays a solid foundation to the engineering students during their first year. It is a multidisciplinary department which always focussed to lay the profound foundation for all Engineering disciplines. The Department has provided an ambience and infrastructure for the students to become researchers and innovators. It imparts valuable education on communicative and technical English, Engineering mathematics, Engineering Physics and Engineering Chemistry. Under S & H, the Department of English conducts Language lab for the students to strengthen their communication and soft skills. The Physics and Chemistry laboratory are fully equipped to help the students learn the experiments and protocols; crystal-clearly to ensure our students to become a outstanding engineers and scientists in future. The Department of Mathematics works hard to assist the students to understand the difficult concepts which will enable them in their pursuit of reasoning, analytical and application skills.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a center of excellence in Humanities & Science by providing high-quality technical education with the necessary skills and values to meet the demands of the industry and society.

Mission

  • To create a close-to-perfect place for the learning process by adopting efficient teaching methodologies and strategies in Humanities & Science.
  • To develop a platform for research by supporting innovative ideas for Research and Development based on the needs of the Industry and Society.
  • To provide career development and higher studies assistance to the student community.
  • To inculcate the demanded skills for modern technologies with ethical and human values.

Head of Department Message

Education is not the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think. Our department is committed to nurturing analytical thinking and scientific temperament in our students.

We provide a supportive environment that facilitates the transition from school to professional engineering education.

Laboratories

The Department of Humanities and Science maintains well-equipped laboratories that serve as the foundation for all engineering disciplines. Our facilities provide first-year students with essential practical knowledge in basic sciences and communication skills.

  • Physics Laboratory: Enables students to explore the fundamental principles of optics, electronics, and solid-state physics through experimental verification.
  • Chemistry Laboratory: Provides hands-on training in chemical analysis, titration methods, and instrumental techniques for material characterization.
  • Communication Skills Laboratory: Focuses on enhancing English language proficiency, public speaking, and corporate communication through interactive software.
  • Foundational Computing Laboratory: Introduction to programming logic and office automation tools, preparing students for advanced engineering computing.
  • Mathematics Laboratory: Utilizes computational tools and mathematical software to visualize complex concepts and solve engineering problems.
